Retrograde

A time-inversion puzzle. Run each site forward, step through the turnstile, then live the same seconds backwards. Your forward self keeps walking, in reverse, right beside you. What you borrow in forward time, you repay inverted.

You are Noor Idris, a sweeper. The Deep Lab under Halvard leaked retrograde matter, and someone has been seeding the city with it. Every site you clear closes one fold in the timeline. Clear them all and you find out who is doing the seeding.
Forward. Reach the turnstile before the timer runs out. The longer you wait before inverting, the more inverted time you get.
Inverted. The clock runs backwards to zero. Reach the blue exit before it does. Bullets fly back into their guns. Doors are open only when someone is really on the switch at that moment.
Credit. A closed door with a switch can be opened on credit: a future inverted you promises to hold the switch. The red bar on the timeline is that promise. Inverted, be on the switch before the clock reaches it, and stay until it passes. Break the promise and the timeline folds.
Reciprocity. Any switch you hold forward is also held at those seconds for your inverted self. Gold marks on the timeline show when.
